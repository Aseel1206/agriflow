SCOPE DECISION (for the 3-day hackathon MVP):

Only the AI price recommendation model targets the demo. It plugs into the
`MockPriceTradeService` / `LightGBMPriceTradeService` swap point defined in idea.txt §9,
grounded in real Agmarknet/data.gov.in mandi price data as the baseline.

Future market price prediction and farmer<->buyer matching remain OUT of scope for
the MVP demo and are stretch goals only if time permits. They must be built against
the already-defined contract endpoints so they can be plugged in later without
changing frontend or business logic:

    POST /ai/price/predict
    POST /ai/trade/score
    POST /ai/logistics/optimize
    POST /ai/freshness/predict

Do not let these become blockers for the hackathon build (see idea.txt §29).

UPDATE — demand forecasting is now IN scope, ahead of schedule: a real trained
LightGBM model (regional mandi arrivals, 748 trees, ~638k historical rows spanning
2001-2021) was contributed and integrated as its own contract endpoint,
POST /ai/demand/predict (plus a convenience GET /market/demand-forecast for all seed
crops at once). This is a genuine trained model, not a mock — see
backend/app/services/demand_forecast_service.py for what it actually predicts
(regional arrivals, a market-activity proxy, NOT a literal count of AgriFlow buyer
orders) and its data-availability caveats (no real recent-arrivals feed to seed its
most important feature, so it's seeded from this platform's own current listing
volume instead; two of five seed crops — Banana, Cabbage — aren't in its training
vocabulary). Surfaced on the Market page as a clearly separate "Regional Market
Outlook (Forecast)" section — never merged with the live Supply vs. Demand numbers.

UPDATE — the AI price recommendation model is now backed by a real trained model
too, closing out both item 1 and (partially) item 5 above: a second LightGBM
regressor (162 trees, same ~638k-row mandi dataset) forecasting *next-day* mandi
price per crop. It doesn't replace the platform's own quality-grade/farmer-expectation
logic in `LightGBMPriceTradeService.predict_price()` — it replaces that logic's
baseline, which used to be a static mandi-snapshot average, with this trained
forecast blended 70/30 with today's price. See
backend/app/services/price_forecast_model.py for the model wrapper and its honesty
caveats (same data-availability caveats as the demand model — no live arrivals/price
history feed, so those features are seeded from platform data — and the same
8-crop training vocabulary gap for Banana/Cabbage, surfaced via lower confidence
rather than hidden). `MockPriceTradeService` (the old static-snapshot-only logic)
remains in price_trade_service.py as the fallback when no db session is available.

AI price recommendation
Model: LightGBM / XGBoost
Input: crop, market, location, historical prices, arrivals, season, demand, etc.
Output: recommended price + confidence/range.
Future market price prediction
Model: LightGBM
Later: Temporal Fusion Transformer if you get enough data.
Output: price forecast for 1/3/7 days.
Demand forecasting
Model: LightGBM
Input: historical demand/orders, crop, location, season, day, weather, festivals, price.
Output: expected quantity demanded.
Farmer ↔ Buyer matching
Model: LightGBM ranking/classification
Input: price compatibility, quantity, distance, quality, shelf life, delivery time.

SCOPE DECISION (for the 3-day hackathon MVP):

Only the AI price recommendation model targets the demo. It plugs into the
`MockPriceTradeService` / `LightGBMPriceTradeService` swap point defined in idea.txt §9,
grounded in real Agmarknet/data.gov.in mandi price data as the baseline.

Future market price prediction, demand forecasting, and farmer<->buyer matching are
explicitly OUT of scope for the MVP demo and are stretch goals only if time permits.
They must be built against the already-defined contract endpoints so they can be
plugged in later without changing frontend or business logic:

    POST /ai/price/predict
    POST /ai/trade/score
    POST /ai/logistics/optimize
    POST /ai/freshness/predict

Do not let these become blockers for the hackathon build (see idea.txt §29).
